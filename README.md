I hold a Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from the University of Toledo and work at the intersection of machine learning, nonlinear dynamical systems, mathematical biology, and scientific computing. ## Selected Projects

### Autoencoder and Topological Data Analysis for Dynamical-System Data

Developed a Python-based pipeline combining neural autoencoders, PCA, t-SNE, Isomap-UMAP Mapper graphs, persistent homology, Betti-number computation, and polynomial-kernel SVR to compress high-dimensional ODE trajectory data and study its latent geometric and topological structure.

### Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Gene Regulatory Networks

Developed PINN-based models with learnable feature maps for inverse problems in gene regulatory networks, comparing parameter recovery against least-squares methods under noisy trajectory data.

### Spatial Epidemic Modeling and Pattern Formation

Built and analyzed a diffusive epidemic model with infection-dependent recovery to study nonlinear recovery behavior, spatial spread, and pattern formation. This work was published in *Mathematical Biosciences*.

### Delayed Predator-Prey Dynamics and Periodic Behavior

Analyzed a delayed predator-prey model using simulation, stability analysis, and global Hopf bifurcation methods to classify periodic orbit structures. This work was published in the *European Journal of Applied Mathematics*.

### Density-Dependent Incidence and Post-Infection Mortality

Studied epidemic models with density-dependent incidence and post-infection mortality to understand how nonlinear transmission assumptions affect outbreak behavior and population-level outcomes.

### Florida Alligator Population Modeling and Data Analysis

Supervised a student team using data-driven population modeling to study Florida alligator trends, validate quantitative findings, and prepare conference-ready research presentations.

### High-Codimension Bifurcation Analysis in Harvested Predator-Prey Models

Used symbolic computation, numerical simulation, and parameter-space analysis to study high-codimension bifurcations, stability transitions, and nonlinear regime changes in harvested predator-prey systems.
